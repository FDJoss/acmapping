How to edit
  • Drag a white point to reshape the track.
  • Double-click the road to add a point there.
  • Right-click a point, or select it and press Del, to remove it.
  • Drag the dots in the Elevation chart to make hills and dips.
  • Select a point in a corner and add banking to tilt it.
  • Ctrl+click points to pick several, Shift+click to pick every point between two, or Shift+drag a box around them. Then use + / − to raise, lower or bank them all at once. Ctrl+A selects every point.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag the grass to pan, F fits the track.
  • Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo. Your work is autosaved in this browser.
